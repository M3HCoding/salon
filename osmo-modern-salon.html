FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?
We offer a wide range of grooming services including haircuts, beard trimming, shaving, hair spa, facials, cleanup, hair coloring, head massage, D-Tan, kids haircut, bridal groom, and groom packages.
Do I need to book an appointment?
Yes, we recommend booking an appointment in advance to ensure availability, especially for premium services and weekend slots. You can book directly through our website or WhatsApp.
What are your working hours?
We are open Monday through Sunday from 08:00 AM to 09:30 PM. We're here to serve you every day of the week.
Do you offer discounts for students?
Yes, we offer a 10% student discount on all services with a valid student ID. Please mention this when booking your appointment.
What safety measures do you follow?
We maintain the highest hygiene standards with sanitized equipment, disposable liners, regular disinfection, and a clean AC waiting area. Your safety is our priority.
